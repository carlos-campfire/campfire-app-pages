What happens behind the button

  1. Campfire
    User clicks Pay with provider on the bill. Campfire POSTs the bill payload to the app's webhook.
    POST /hooks/pay-bill {bill_id, vendor, amount, currency}
  2. Your app
    Validates vendor bank details against the approved record, then calls the provider's payouts API.
    POST /api/v1/transfers
  3. Provider
    Creates a pending transfer and sends a webhook back when it settles.
    transfer.status = PENDING → SETTLED
  4. Your app → Campfire
    Writes the bill payment through the OAuth connection (scope bill_payments:write). Bill flips to Paid.
    POST /api/bill-payments
