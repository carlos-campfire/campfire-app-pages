Campfire app · AP Payment Bridge

Set up a new supplier for payment in Airwallex

Airwallex holds the supplier's bank details and runs KYB. Campfire stays the ledger of record: once the supplier exists in Airwallex, bills for them can be paid straight from the bill page.

Open Airwallex — Create vendor ↗
Sandbox environment (Datavine Demo). Opens in a new tab.
What happens next
  1. 1
    Create the vendor in Airwallex with bank details and payment terms. Airwallex validates the account.
  2. 2
    Airwallex sends a vendor.created webhook to the app; the app upserts the payee in Campfire via the API with the Airwallex ID as external_id.
  3. 3
    From then on, bills for that payee show Pay with provider, and payments flow back as bill payments.
